# Assignment Notes

## Product Framing

The assignment is not asking for a generic conference CRM. Grain sells to PSPs, cross-border payment companies, travel wholesalers, and teams with FX exposure, so the tool prioritizes sales judgment:

- Which events concentrate Grain's ICP?
- Which events deserve reps?
- Which repeated contacts are warming up?
- Which leads should move into HubSpot quickly?

## Financial And Sales Terms

- PSP: payment service provider. Strong Grain target because PSPs often move money across currencies.
- FX exposure: risk that exchange-rate movement changes revenue, cost, margin, or cash position.
- Treasury: the finance function that manages cash, liquidity, payments, and risk.
- ICP: ideal customer profile.
- Pipeline generation: activities that create future sales opportunities.
- Lead qualification: deciding whether a contact has pain, authority, urgency, and fit.

## Scope Chosen

This is a single-user MVP with seeded public conference data and LocalStorage persistence. That keeps the tool simple enough to deploy and demo while still showing the end-to-end workflow.

## Why This Should Score Well

- Sales empathy: capture flow is short and mobile-first.
- AI judgment: AI is used where summarization and language generation are genuinely helpful.
- Cross-conference intelligence: repeat contacts get interpretation, not just a duplicate count.
- Shipping instinct: HubSpot and OpenAI work with keys, but demo safely without them.
